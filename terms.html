Terms for using Dabbli

These terms describe the parent or guardian’s responsibilities when a family uses Dabbli.

Who may use Dabbli

A parent or legal guardian must create and control the parent account, make the app’s privacy choices, and supervise the child’s use. Dabbli does not provide child accounts. The parent confirms that they have the authority to make these choices for the child using the app.

What Dabbli provides

Dabbli offers parent-guided creative, thinking, exploration and learning challenges. Challenges invite effort and conversation; they are not professional educational, medical, psychological or safety advice. Parents decide whether a challenge is suitable for their child and surroundings.

Parent supervision and safe use

The parent remains responsible for supervision, choosing a safe place, checking materials, and stopping any activity that is not appropriate. The child should not use Dabbli to photograph or record other people without permission or to create unlawful, harmful or private material.

Local family data

Child work, reviews, scores and points remain on the iPhone and are not synchronised to Dabbli’s servers. They may be permanently lost if the app is uninstalled, local family data is deleted, or the device is lost or damaged. The parent is responsible for reviewing anything they want to keep before deletion.

Accounts and security

The parent must use an email address they control, protect access to that mailbox and device, keep the Parent PIN private, and notify Dabbli of suspected unauthorised account use. One-time codes, passwords, PINs and recovery information must never be sent to Dabbli support.

Points and availability

Dabbli points are an in-app family feature. They have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and do not create a payment obligation. We may maintain, improve, suspend or discontinue parts of the service. We aim to preserve compatible local data during supported updates but cannot promise uninterrupted or error-free availability.

Content and acceptable use

Dabbli’s app, challenge catalogue, branding and software are protected by applicable intellectual-property laws. Families receive a personal, limited and non-transferable right to use the app as provided. Do not misuse the service, interfere with security, scrape the catalogue, or attempt unauthorised access.

Ending use

A parent can stop using Dabbli, delete local family data and request deletion of the parent account through the app. We may restrict service access when reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance or serious misuse, while respecting applicable rights.

Responsibility and applicable law

Nothing in these terms excludes rights or responsibilities that cannot legally be excluded. Subject to those protections, Dabbli is provided without guarantees beyond those expressly required by law. Contact and changes

Questions can be sent to dabbli.support@gmail.com. Material changes will be communicated appropriately and, where needed, require renewed parent acknowledgement.
