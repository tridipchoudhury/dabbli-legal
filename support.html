Parent and guardian support

How can we help?

Email support@dabbli.app from the registered parent email address when possible.

Please do not attach child work. Never send a child’s answers, photos or audio recordings. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, the Parent PIN, API keys or recovery information.

Helpful details to include

  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • The Dabbli app version shown in Parent Settings.
  • The support ID shown in Parent Settings, if available. It is a technical reference and cannot unlock the app.
  • The approximate date and time of the problem.
  • Your mobile operating-system version, without including a device serial number.

Sign-in codes

A requested code can take a few minutes to arrive and may appear in the spam or junk folder. Request only one code at a time and use the newest valid code. Dabbli support will never ask you to forward or disclose a one-time code.

Account and privacy requests

Use the in-app controls for usage sharing, local family-data deletion and parent-account deletion where possible. The account-deletion page explains how to request deletion without the app, what is deleted and the limited retention that applies. For access, correction, restriction, objection, portability or another privacy question, email the same address. Dabbli may verify the request through the registered parent email.

If child material is sent accidentally

Access will be restricted, the material will not be forwarded, and it will be deleted within seven days after identification. Dabbli does not need child work to investigate ordinary technical problems.

Response and retention

Dabbli is operated by an independent developer, so immediate support is not guaranteed. Ordinary resolved support correspondence is deleted within 90 days unless a bounded legal or security reason requires it to be kept longer.

Urgent situations

Dabbli support is not an emergency service. For an immediate safety or medical concern, contact the appropriate local emergency or professional service.
