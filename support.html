Parent support

How can we help?

Email dabbli.support@gmail.com from the parent account’s email address when possible.

Please do not attach child work. Never send a child’s answers, photos or recordings. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, the Parent PIN or recovery information.

Helpful details to include

  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • The Dabbli app version shown in Parent Settings.
  • The support ID shown in Parent Settings, if it is available. It is a technical reference and cannot unlock the app.
  • The approximate date and time of the problem.

Account and privacy requests

Use the in-app controls for usage-sharing choices, local family-data deletion and parent-account deletion where possible. For access, correction or other privacy questions, email the same address. We may need to verify the request through the registered parent email.

If child material is sent accidentally

Access will be restricted and the material will be deleted within seven days after it is identified. Dabbli support does not need child work to investigate ordinary technical problems.

About urgent situations

Dabbli support is not an emergency service. For an immediate safety or medical concern, contact the appropriate local emergency or professional service.
