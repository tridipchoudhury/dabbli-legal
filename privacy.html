Your family’s privacy in Dabbli

This notice explains what stays on your iPhone, what Dabbli’s service processes, why it is used, and the choices available to parents.

Important: child answers, photos, recordings, reviews, scores and points stay on the family’s iPhone. Dabbli does not upload that material to its servers.

Who is responsible

Dabbli is operated by Tridip Choudhury in the Netherlands. For privacy questions or requests, email dabbli.support@gmail.com.

Information kept on the iPhone

Dabbli keeps the child’s optional nickname, parent-selected age, challenge history, written answers, photos, recordings, reviews, scores and points in protected app storage on the family’s iPhone. Dabbli does not receive this information on its backend.

This local information is not synchronised between devices and is excluded from iCloud backup. Uninstalling Dabbli, deleting local family data, or losing the device can permanently remove it.

Information processed by Dabbli’s service

  • The parent’s email address, needed to create and access the parent account and send one-time sign-in codes.
  • Account, session, security and acknowledgement records needed to run and protect the service.
  • Optional, pseudonymous product-usage events only when the parent actively enables usage sharing. These events never contain child answers, photos, recordings, nickname, scores or points.
  • Limited operational diagnostics that use safe technical codes and do not contain child work or free-form answers.
  • Support correspondence that a parent chooses to send, together with the app’s pseudonymous support ID when supplied.

Why this information is used

Parent-account and service information is used to provide and secure Dabbli, deliver requested one-time codes, maintain content and honour privacy choices. Optional usage information is used only to understand whether approved product features work and are useful. The detailed lawful-basis assessment will be confirmed before publication.

Service providers and locations

Dabbli uses carefully selected providers for application hosting and databases, transactional email, protected administration, public legal pages, support email, and Apple app distribution. The final provider and international-transfer description will be completed after the production accounts and agreements are reviewed.

How long information is kept

  • Consumed or expired one-time-code records: up to 24 hours.
  • Expired or revoked session metadata: up to 30 days.
  • Operational logs and essential diagnostics: up to 30 days.
  • Optional raw product-usage events: up to 90 days.
  • Anonymous, thresholded weekly product totals: up to 12 months.
  • Resolved support correspondence: up to 90 days.
  • Encrypted backend backups: a rolling maximum of 30 days.
  • A minimal account-deletion replay record: up to 35 days, without the email address or child information.

Your choices and rights

A parent can leave optional usage sharing off or withdraw it later, inspect parent-account information, correct an email through the supported process, delete the parent account, or delete all local family data. Depending on the request and applicable law, parents may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability for applicable backend parent data.

Dabbli cannot provide a backend export of child work because it never receives that work. Parents can inspect it on the iPhone before local deletion. Requests may require proportionate verification through the registered parent email.

Support and child information

Please do not email a child’s answers, photos or recordings. If such material is accidentally received, access is restricted and it is deleted within seven days after identification.

Complaints and updates

Contact Dabbli first so we can try to resolve a concern. Parents also have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority or another competent supervisory authority. Material changes to this notice will be presented appropriately in the app.
